Hello! I'm Hung Pham, a CS undergraduate student at George Mason University. I'm an aspiring undergraduate researcher interested in the intersection of Machine Learning System and Security. Lately, I've been diving into the security leakage in large language models (LLMs). I'm focused on finding vulnerabilities in LLMs optimization method such as KV caching, pruning, and speculative decoding that could increase the risk of leaking sensitive data.

I've had the opportunity work as an undergraduate research assistant at the Security of Emerging Computer System And Technologies Lab (SECSAT), under the mentorship of [Dr. Xiaokuan Zhang](https://mainarke.github.io/) and PhD student Vishnu Teja Kandalam. My research focuses on identifying security risk cause by LLM optimization techniques, and I am on the path of writing a first author research paper.

I've also been engaged in the [Robotic Anticipatory Intelligence and Learning Group (RAIL)](https://cs.gmu.edu/~gjstein/people/), working under [Dr. Gregory Stein](https://gjstein.com/) and PhD student [Abhishek Paudel](https://abpaudel.com/), where I focus in the machine learning aspect of robotics. My work in the lab involve integrating learning planner into LoCoBot hardware to test navigation in an unknown indoor environment. This work has allowed developed a better grasp on machine learning system.
