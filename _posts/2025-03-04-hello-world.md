### Hello World!

Every CS-related blogger always has a "Hello World!" post, and it’s really hard to break the tradition.

Welcome to my blog! This space will be where I share my insights on **machine learning**, my research directions, and the occasional rare gem I stumble across. I believe that the sharing of knowledges can help me grasp concepts more deeply. The idea of a blog hit me when my advisor pointed out that a PhD isn’t just about research; it’s also about communicating through writing (who knew!). So, I’m here to improve my writing skills, and I hope you’ll see them get better with each post. Thank you for reading, be sure to share your thoughts down below.
